The model can detect proximal Femur-Hip fracture from regular image of xrays with decent precision. The model was trained on yolo v9s model available at https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolov9/#yolov9-benchmarks , and to meet the training hardaware requirements the training was done Kaggle notebook available at https://www.kaggle.com/code/sovan1033/fracture-detection-using-yolov9 .
